Zine mazes

Before the logo was a logo, it was a snake in a hand-drawn zine of maze puzzles. Tracing a maze and routing a trace are the same thing.

Time 0.0 sTrace 0 px

luci3D: Start at the filled arrow, finish at the pink square by the hollow one. Arrow keys or WASD (click the maze first). On a phone, use the pad.

The full game is in development: board cities to explore, five guilds to play, and luci3D as your guide.
