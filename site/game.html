Zine mazes

Before the snake was a logo, it lived in a hand-drawn zine of maze puzzles. Solving a maze and routing a trace turn out to be the same job.

Time 0.0 sTrace 0 px

luci3D: Start at the filled arrow and route your trace to the pink square by the hollow one. Arrow keys or WASD (click the maze first). On a phone, use the pad.

The full game is in the works: board cities to explore, five guilds to choose from, and luci3D showing you the way to the Lab.
