The Lab

Where our boards get built, fixed and tested before they ship. Small batches from Canada; pay by card (Stripe) or PayPal.

U2

From the line

Our PCB maker places most of the parts. We solder the video decoder and the microphone by hand, then test every board on a camera and pack each kit ourselves.

Small batches mean the boards you get were checked one by one.

Questions

When can I get one?

The DIY kit is at the prototype stage. When a batch has been built and tested, it goes on sale here. Hit "Get notified" to hear first.

What else do I need?

A Luckfox Pico Pro or Max, a microSD card and an analog FPV camera. The kit takes video and 5 V from your flight controller.

Is the software open?

Yes. The recorder is open source (GPL-3.0) and on GitHub.
