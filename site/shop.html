Shop

Open hardware in small batches, shipped from Canada. Pay by card (Stripe) or PayPal. Every board's files are on GitHub, so you can always build your own.

Questions

Why can't I buy anything yet?

The first board is at the prototype stage. Once a batch has been built and tested, it goes on sale here. Hit "Get notified" to hear about it.

Can I build my own?

Yes. The KiCad files, Gerbers and parts lists are on GitHub.
