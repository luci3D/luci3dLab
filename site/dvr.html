Open DVR

An open recorder for analog FPV video. Stage 1 upcycles a DVR board anyone can buy; stage 2 is our own hardware, running the same recorder.

Download release 0.9Source on GitHub

Stage 1: the upcycled DVR

For the BAYCKRC / COOAI "MINI DVR" (Goke GK7202V300 SoC and Techpoint TP2850 decoder). Its stock firmware encodes one field of each frame twice and drops the other, and its microphone drifts out of sync. Our recorder fixes both.

  • Every field recorded: real 50 fps PAL and 59.94 fps NTSC, PAL/NTSC detected automatically
  • H.264 in MPEG-TS, 5-minute clips that survive a power cut
  • The board's microphone as an MP3 track, in sync within one frame
  • Button and LEDs work; settings in the stock config.txt
  • Nothing is flashed: delete the files and the stock firmware is back

Get it running

  1. Download open-dvr-0.9.zip from the Releases page.
  2. Extract it into the top folder of the board's SD card.
  3. Power on.

The zip has its own README.txt with the button, the LEDs and every setting.

Stage 2: our own hardware

One recorder codebase on the Rockchip RV1103, with a Techpoint decoder for the analog video. The DVR never sits in the live video path: it taps the camera line in parallel, so it adds zero latency, and you keep your video even if the DVR crashes.
