open-dvr

An open-source recorder for analog FPV. It started as new firmware for a cheap DVR board you can already buy, and it's moving onto our own hardware.

Download release 0.9Source on GitHub

Release 0.9: the upcycled DVR

Runs on the BAYCKRC / COOAI "MINI DVR" (Goke GK7202V300 with a Techpoint TP2850 decoder). Its stock firmware encodes one field of every frame twice and throws the other away, and its audio drifts. open-dvr fixes both, straight from the SD card.

  • Every field recorded: PAL 960 x 576 at 50 fps, NTSC 960 x 480 at 59.94 fps, detected automatically
  • H.264 in MPEG-TS, 5-minute clips that survive a power cut
  • On-board microphone as an MP3 track, in sync within one frame
  • microSD cards up to 512 GB
  • Nothing gets flashed: delete the files and the stock firmware is back

Get it running

  1. Download open-dvr-0.9.zip from the Releases page.
  2. Extract it into the top folder of the board's SD card.
  3. Power on.

The zip has its own README.txt with the button, the LEDs and every setting.
